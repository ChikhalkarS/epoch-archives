# Epoch Archives Studio
## Indian City Series

**YouTube Channel**: `epocharchivesco`
**Channel Name**: Epoch Archives Studio

### Overview
Animated history videos focused on Indian cities, expanding to general history content.

### Project Structure (~/Movies/Epoch-Archives)
```
Epoch-Archives/
├── assets/
│   ├── audio/           # Voiceovers, sound effects, music
│   ├── fonts/           # Custom fonts for animation style
│   └── images/          # Research images, historical photos, maps
├── episodes/            # Individual city episode videos
├── output/              # Final rendered videos
├── scripts/             # Episode scripts, outlines
└── research/            # Sources, references by city
```

### Launch Plan: Indian City Series
1. Delhi (Ancient Dilli to Modern Capital)
2. Mumbai (From seven islands to financial hub)
3. Jaipur (The Pink City heritage)
4. Varanasi (City of Light, spiritual axis)
5. Hampi (Lost city of Vijayanagara)

### Naming Convention
- Episodes: `episode-01-delhi-history.mkv`
- Scripts: `delhi-script-v1.md`
- Research: `delhi-sources.md`
