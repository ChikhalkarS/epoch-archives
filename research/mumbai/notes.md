## Story arc
- Founding: seven islands, Koli fishers, Mumba Devi (F1, F2, F7)
- Rulers / turning points: Gujarat Sultanate -> Portuguese 1534 (F6) -> dowry 1661 (F8) -> Crown 1665 (F10) -> East India Company 1668, £10 rent (F12) -> Aungier, HQ from Surat 1687 (F14, F15)
- Wars / sieges: not central; freedom struggle instead - Congress 1885 (F27), Quit India 1942 (F28)
- Trade / economy: cotton boom and 1865 crash (F23, F25); first train 1853 (F22)
- Culture / food: Mahalakshmi legend (F20); Lady Avabai's toll-free causeway (F21). Food skipped for runtime.
- Geography (why here?): islands joined by reclamation - Hornby Vellard 1782-84, Colaba causeway 1838, Mahim causeway 1845 (F16, F18, F19, F21)
- Strange facts: dowry city; £10 rent; Congress session moved due to cholera; a woman funded a toll-free causeway; the city still reclaims land (Coastal Road 2024, F33, F34)

## Twist (minute 4-6)
The city wasn't really "given" as a city: it was seven islands and swamp, and the king rented it out for £10 a year because it was a burden. Then the real twist: much of today's Mumbai was sea - the city was taken from the sea, and it still is (Coastal Road 2024).
