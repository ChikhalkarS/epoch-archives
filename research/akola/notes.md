## Story arc
- Geography (why here?): black-soil plain of Berar, Morna river through the city; perfect cotton land (F1, F2, F34).
- Founding: the name - two views (F3) and the Akolsingh legend with the Rajeshwar temple and the queen (F4, F5).
- Rulers / turning points: Khalji -> Bahmani -> Nizam Shahi -> Mughals (F12, F13); Aurangzeb's minister Asad Khan builds the stone fort, Asadgad, 1697 (F8, F9); Nizam of Hyderabad takes Berar 1724 (F15).
- Forts around the city: Narnala in the tiger forest (F19-F22), Balapur's river-moat fort (F16-F18).
- Wars / sieges: Battle of Argaon, November 1803 - Wellesley vs Raghoji II Bhonsle; treaty of Deogaon (F23-F27); 12 years later the same man wins Waterloo (F28).
- Trade / economy: Nizam's debt -> Berar handed to the British in 1853 (F29); Akola HQ of West Berar (F30); American Civil War -> cotton rush (F31); railway in the 1860s carries cotton to Bombay (F32, F33); "Cotton City" (F34); 1903 permanent lease (F35).
- Culture / food: Varhadi rassa, zunka-bhakar, thecha (F37).
- Strange facts: queen-in-the-lingam legend (F5); a fort dismantled by the British whose hall became a school (F11); Narnala inside a tiger reserve, highest point of the district (F22); Balapur fort protected by floods, then partly felled by rain in 2025 (F17, F18).
- Modern link: still a cotton town; 18 lakh people in the district (F36).

## Twist (the "aha" moment for minute 4-6)
Akola's fate was decided twice by events far away: a debt in Hyderabad (1853) gave it to the British, and a war in America (1861-65) made its cotton suddenly precious - the railway arrived to carry Varhad's cotton to the ships at Bombay. The "Cotton City" was born from someone else's war.
