Status rules:
- **verified**: 2+ independent reliable sources agree.
- **disputed**: sources disagree. Script must say "kuch itihaskar maante hain..." and give both views.
- **legend**: folk story. Script must say "kehte hain..." / "lok-katha ke anusaar...".
- **unverified**: do not use in the script.
