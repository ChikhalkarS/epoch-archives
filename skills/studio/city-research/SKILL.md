# City research

Accuracy first. The script may only use facts that are in the fact sheet with a source.

1. Copy `research/_template/` to `research/<slug>/`.
2. Search the web. Priority sources: ASI, UNESCO, state gazetteers and tourism/government pages, Census, university/museum pages, books on Google Books/Archive.org, reputable newspapers (The Hindu, Indian Express, Times of India features), JSTOR abstracts. Wikipedia only to find primary citations.
3. Cover: founding and name origin, rulers and turning points, wars/sieges, trade and economy, culture and food, geography (why the city is here), 3-5 strange facts, one modern-day link.
4. Fill the fact sheet. Each fact: one line, source IDs, status (verified / disputed / legend / unverified). Dates: give the source's exact wording; if sources differ, mark disputed.
5. In notes.md: 3 hook questions (each answerable in the video), the twist, and a teaser that links to the next city in `tracker/videos.csv`.
6. Sensitive topics (religion, caste, Partition, communal violence, contested sites): neutral wording, both views, no blame on present-day communities. Flag them for the owner.
7. Keep it compact: about 25-40 facts. No long copied paragraphs (copyright); paraphrase.
