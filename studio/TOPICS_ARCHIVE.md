## Where the real files live (for deep research)
- **National Archives of India**: Abhilekh Patal (abhilekh-patal.in), digitised files and notifications.
- **Indian Culture portal** (indianculture.gov.in): gazetteers, rare books, archival stories.
- **Parliament debates** (eparlib.nic.in): e.g. the 1954 French India transfer debates.
- **Court judgments**: Indian Kanoon / Casemine, e.g. the 2003 Calcutta High Court PIL on Kolkata's founding.
- **National Library of Scotland, India Papers** (digital.nls.uk): plague reports, sanitary reports.
- **Archive.org**: Imperial Gazetteer, old city histories (Cotton's *Calcutta Old and New*, Love's *Vestiges of Old Madras*).
- **British Library and UK National Archives catalogues**: East India Company records.
These hosts are added to the session's allowed sites only when a topic is picked.
