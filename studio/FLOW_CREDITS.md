# Google Flow credit costs (official page, checked 2026-11-27)
Source: https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16526234 ("Limits are subject to change"; the current cost shows in Flow → prompt box → Settings).

| Model | 4 s | 6 s | 8 s | 10 s | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Veo 3.1 Lite** (720p) | 10 | 10 | 10 | n/a | Flat per clip. Ultra: 5. **Our default: always 8 s** |
| Omni Flash 1.2, 360p | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 | 1 credit/second |
| Omni Flash 1.2, 720p | 12 | 18 | 24 | 30 | 3 credits/second. 11–15 s and Extend need Plus/Pro/Ultra |
| Veo 3.1 Fast / Quality | 20 / – | 20 / – | 20 / 100 | – | **Retired Dec 5, 2026** |

- Credits per month: free = 50/day (no rollover). Plus +200/month, Pro +1,000, Ultra +10,000 (Ultra 20x: +25,000).
- Credits are per *generation*: a request that makes 2 videos costs double.
- Veo Extend was retired on Nov 5, 2026.
