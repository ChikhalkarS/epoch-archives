## ⚠️ Key finding: the rules change on Feb 1, 2027
Your "8,000 watch hours" figure was **not wrong**. It is the **new** entry rule for channels that apply from Feb 1, 2027 [P2]:
> "Starting Feb. 1, 2027, YPP entry thresholds for new creators are changing to 8,000 qualified watch hours in the last 365 days, or 20M qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days, in addition to still needing 1k subscribers. If you are already in YPP, your status is not impacted by this update."

## Full tier (ad revenue, Premium): thresholds
| | Until Jan 31, 2027 [P1] | From Feb 1, 2027 [P2] |
|---|---|---|
| Subscribers | 1,000 | 1,000 |
| Long-form route | 4,000 qualified public watch hours, last 12 months | **8,000** qualified watch hours, last 365 days |
| Shorts route | 10M qualified Shorts views, last 90 days | **20M** qualified Shorts views, last 90 days |

- Shorts-feed watch hours **do not count** toward the watch-hour threshold [P1]: "any qualified watch hours from Shorts views in the Shorts Feed won't count towards the 4,000 qualified public watch hours threshold."
- These don't count either: private, unlisted or deleted videos, ad campaigns, and Shorts [P1].
- Meeting the threshold ≠ automatic entry. There is a review of the whole channel [P1].

## Earlier tier (fan funding, Shopping, Creator Partnerships): no ad revenue
- [P2]: "500 subscribers and either 3,000 qualified watch hours in the last year or 3M qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days". This is unchanged after Feb 1, 2027.
- The "3 public uploads in 90 days" condition you mentioned is **not stated** on the Feb-2027 page. Check it in YouTube Studio → Earn when you get close.
- It unlocks memberships, Super Chat/Thanks and Shopping. It does **not** unlock ad revenue.

## Other requirements [P1]
Live in an eligible region (India: yes [P4]). No active Community Guidelines strikes. 2-Step Verification on. Advanced features access. One active AdSense for YouTube account. Follow the channel monetization policies [P5]. For us, that means original commentary and research, not reused content, and every video needs real added value.

## Also changing Feb 1, 2027 [P2], [P3]
- Shorts Creator Pool earnings require **10M qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days** each month (even after you're in YPP).
- Active-channel rule: 1,000 watch hours/365 days, OR 1M Shorts views/90 days, OR **2 long videos or 5 Shorts every 90 days**.
- Partners must accept the updated terms by **Jan 31, 2027**.

## What this means for the goal "monetized before Feb 2027"
- Today is Nov 27, 2026, so there are **~9 weeks** left to *apply under the old rules* (1,000 subs + 4,000 hrs, or 10M Shorts views).
- From a new or small channel, that is very unlikely. 4,000 hrs ≈ 24,000 views of a 10-min video at ~50% retention, plus 1,000 subs, within 9 weeks.
- **Realistic plan:**
  1. Push hard until Jan 31 anyway (1 long + 4 Shorts/week). If a Short breaks out, the 10M Shorts route is the only real chance.
  2. Plan for the **new** rule: 1,000 subs + 8,000 watch hrs in 365 days. Long-form drives this, so make long episodes the priority. Aim for 10-min EPs and ≥ 40% retention.
  3. The earlier tier (500 subs + 3,000 hrs) is still a reachable 2027 milestone (fan funding / Shopping).
- **Math for 8,000 hrs:** at 10-min EPs and 45% average view duration ≈ 4.5 min/view, that's ≈ 107k long-form views in 12 months. At 1 EP/week (~50 EPs), that's ≈ 2,150 views per EP on average.

**Owner decision needed:** confirm the goal becomes "8,000 hrs + 1,000 subs within 12 months of EP1", with "monetized before Feb 2027" kept as a stretch target.
