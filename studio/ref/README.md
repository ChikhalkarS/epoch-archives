# Reference ingredients (make once, reuse in every episode)
Built with the `character-consistency` skill. Make them in Flow (Image / Whisk, or Nano Banana if your Flow shows it), pick the best of several candidates, and save each one as a Flow ingredient.

## 1. Sutradhar close-up (pick the one you'd recognise in a crowd)
```
Ultra-detailed 2D hand-drawn cartoon portrait of SUTRADHAR, an elderly Indian storyteller, vintage noir storybook style, bold clean black ink outlines, cel shading with soft painted shadows.
Chest-up framing, front view, facing camera, plain cool grey background, even soft studio light.
EYES: warm dark-brown eyes with bright highlights behind round brass-rimmed spectacles, lenses catching a glint of lamp light, thick white bushy eyebrows that curl upward at the outer ends, gentle crow's-feet lines.
SKIN: round warm-brown face, rosy cheeks, a broad rounded nose, a few ink wrinkle lines on the forehead.
HAIR: short neat white beard drawn with fine ink strands, white hair visible at the temples, bald crown.
EXPRESSION: closed-mouth knowing half-smile, brows raised a little, as if about to tell a secret.
CLOTHING: deep red woollen shawl wrapped over the shoulders, cream kurta collar visible, the strap of a faded indigo cloth satchel crossing the chest.
TECHNICAL: 2D cartoon illustration, flat cel colours with soft painted shading, sharp lines, high resolution.
```
## 2. Sutradhar sheet (attach the chosen close-up as reference)
```
Character reference sheet of the same 2D cartoon character, 16:9 landscape, three panels side by side on one seamless cool grey studio background.
Left panel (45% width): bust-up front view, facing camera. Center panel: full body standing straight, front view, arms relaxed, feet visible, figure fills 90% of panel height. Right panel: full body rear view, heels visible.
Even studio lighting, identical appearance in all three panels.
IDENTITY: elderly Indian storyteller, round warm-brown face, thick white bushy eyebrows, short neat white beard, round brass-rimmed spectacles, deep red woollen shawl over a cream knee-length kurta, cream churidar trousers, brown leather mojari shoes with curled toes, faded indigo cloth satchel on a strap across the chest, holds a rolled parchment scroll tied with red thread in his right hand, slightly hunched, short and expressive. 2D hand-drawn cartoon, bold black ink outlines, cel shading.
```

Save as ingredient **"Sutradhar sheet"**. In every Sutradhar prompt, add: `The Sutradhar ingredient defines his face, beard, spectacles, shawl and satchel only; ignore its grey background and panel layout.`
