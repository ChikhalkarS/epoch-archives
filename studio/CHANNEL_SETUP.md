# YouTube channel setup (owner does it; Claude never logs in or publishes)

## 0. Account (new email)
1. Sign in to the new Google account and **turn on 2-Step Verification** (myaccount.google.com → Security). YPP requires it.
2. Go to youtube.com → profile icon → **Create a channel**. Use a **brand-style name** (below), not your personal name.
3. YouTube Studio → Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility → **verify your phone number** (unlocks "advanced features", which YPP requires, plus custom thumbnails and long uploads).

## 5. YPP policy checks (the channel is reviewed as a whole)
- **Inauthentic or mass-produced content** is the main risk for AI channels. Every file needs original research, a different story and structure, and sources in the description. Never re-upload the same clips with small changes.
- No copyrighted music or footage. Music comes from the YouTube Audio Library or our synthesised pad.
- No misleading titles. The hook must be paid off in the video.
