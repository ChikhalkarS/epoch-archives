# EP1 research: भोपाल की झीलें (ep01-bhopal-jheel)
Lane: A city · Next EP: ep02-bhopal-begum (the Begums) · Researched 2026-11-27 (builds on `research/bhopal/`; Hoshang Shah status corrected to **disputed**)

## The belief
"Bhopal is the City of Lakes. The lakes are just nature's gift." The Upper Lake is in fact an 11th-century reservoir made by an earthen dam (F1). The Lower Lake is from 1794 (F19).

## Hook options
1. **भोपाल की सबसे बड़ी झील क़ुदरत ने नहीं बनाई।** (belief flip, chosen)
2. एक हज़ार साल पहले, एक नदी को उल्टी दिशा में किसने मोड़ा? (F4)
3. 3 महीने, 3 साल, 30 साल: एक झील को मिटाने की कहानी सच है या ग़लतफ़हमी? (F14–F16)

## Three reveals
1. **The lake is a dam.** An earthen dam on the Kolans turned a small river into a 31 km² lake. The river now drains the other way, into the Kaliasot (F1, F3, F4). Even the city's name may mean "Bhoj's dam" (F7, disputed with F8).
2. **Bhoj built an even bigger lake, and it's gone.** At Bhojpur, ~30 km away, two giant stone dams blocked the Betwa (F9, F10). The north dam still stands. The southern one lies broken in the river (F11). Next to it: an unfinished temple and building drawings carved on rock (F12, F13).
3. **The famous "sultan drained it" story may be a 130-year-old misreading.** Popular account: Hoshang Shah broke the dam (3 months / 3 years / 30 years) (F14). Another view: a monsoon surge broke it in the 11th century (F15). The chronicle's dam may have been **Bhopal's own** lake, where bandits hid on an island (F16, F17). If so, Bhopal's lake was broken once and later raised again (F18).

## Myth check
"होशंगशाह ने भोजपुर की झील तुड़वाकर सुखा दी।" → **विवादित** (F14 vs F15, F16).

## आज भी दिखता है
Takia island in Bhojtal (F17). Raja Bhoj statue on a pillar in the lake, 2011 (F23). Bhadbhada's 11 gates, 1965 (F20). Chhota Talab, 1794 (F19). Bhojpur: the standing north dam, the fallen southern dam, the quarry drawings (F11, F13). Bhoj Wetland Ramsar site, 2002 (F22). The city's drinking water (F21).
